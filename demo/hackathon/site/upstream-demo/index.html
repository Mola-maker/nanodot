What it is这是什么

nanoMuse is one agent on your Android phone, iPhone and iPad, Mac, Windows, Linux and the web. The devices share one account and show the same conversations: say it on the phone, it runs on your PC, and the approval comes back to the device in your hand. nanoMuse 是同一个智能体，在你的 Android 手机、iPhone 和 iPad、Mac、Windows、Linux 和网页上。几台设备共用一个账号，看到同样的对话：在手机上说一句，它在你的电脑上执行，要确认的事回到你手里的这台设备上。

It does things instead of answering questions: a shell, a browser, MCP servers and skills, and with Hands on, the apps on your phone and the windows on your computer through their screens. The agent never runs a tool itself: every call goes through the Sentinel, which stops to ask before deleting, sending or paying. 它做事，而不只是回答：有 shell、浏览器、MCP 服务器和技能；打开「手」之后，还能隔着屏幕操作手机上的应用和电脑上的窗口。智能体自己从不执行工具：每一次调用都经过 Sentinel，删除、发送、付款之前它会先停下来问你。

What it is, what it knows about you and when it wakes are Markdown files you can read and edit. 它是谁、它知道你的什么、它什么时候醒来，都是你能读也能改的 Markdown 文件。

Free, open source, non-profit. Let's build it together. The phone app, the desktop app, the web console and the relay that joins them are one repository under GPL-3.0-or-later. Sign in to the community relay and you get a free allowance of model use and your devices in sync; with a key of your own the runtime needs no account at all, and the same relay runs on a server of yours. 免费、开源、非营利。一起把它做好。手机应用、桌面应用、网页控制台和把它们连起来的中继在同一个仓库里，GPL-3.0-or-later。登录社区中继，有一份免费的模型额度，设备之间互相同步；用自己的 key，运行时完全不需要账号；同一个中继也可以跑在你自己的服务器上。

nanoMuse keeps evolvingnanoMuse 在持续进化

A personal agent should not live only in someone else's cloud, so nanoMuse is the open-source, low-cost alternative: on your own devices, with a model you choose and a relay you can host yourself. We want to build it with developers and users everywhere, so that each person truly owns a personal agent. Open an issue or send a pull request; either one helps nanoMuse get better. 个人智能体不该只长在别人的云里，nanoMuse 想做一个开源、低成本的平替：装在自己的设备上，模型自己选，中继也能自己架。我们希望和全球的开发者、使用者一起，让每个人真正拥有自己的个人智能体。提个 issue 或者发个 PR 都是在帮 nanoMuse 变得更好。

Docs文档

The documentation is at nanomuse.cn/docs. If you are installing, start with Android or the desktop app. Run nanoMuse yourself is the relay and the runtime on a server of yours. The hub is how your devices talk to each other through the relay; the screen as a hand is how the agent sees a screen and acts on it, phone and computer alike. Privacy says what the relay keeps, for how long, and how to delete it. 文档在 nanomuse.cn/docs。要安装的话，从 Android 或桌面应用开始。自己运行 nanoMuse 讲中继和运行时怎么跑在你自己的服务器上。Hub 讲你的几台设备怎么经过中继互相通话；屏幕作为手讲智能体怎么看屏幕、怎么动手，手机和电脑一样。隐私说明中继保存什么、保存多久、怎么删除。
