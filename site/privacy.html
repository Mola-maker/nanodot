PRIVACY隐私

Choose what you send.由你选择发送什么。

This static site does not request a microphone, capture your screen, run model inference or collect project credentials. Language and theme preferences are stored in your browser. No analytics or third-party fonts are loaded.静态站不申请麦克风、不捕获屏幕、不运行模型推理，也不收集项目凭据。语言与外观偏好保存在浏览器内。不加载统计分析或第三方字体。

When using the project gateway使用项目网关时

Screen images and audio are sent to Cloudflare only when you choose to send them through an authorized client. Text, images and audio are processed by the selected allowlisted model. Check the material before sending; do not send private content you do not intend the provider to process.只有你在授权客户端主动发送时，屏幕图片与音频才交给 Cloudflare。文字、图片与音频由所选白名单模型处理。发送前请检查内容，仅发送你愿意交给服务商处理的材料。

The project gateway does not persist request or response payloads or conversation history. Its durable admission gate stores authorized client identifiers, counters and admission times. Diagnostics are limited to request ID, model ID, status, elapsed time and counts; no request body or credential is logged.项目网关不持久存储请求或响应 payload，也不保存对话历史。持久准入门仅保存授权客户端标识、计数与准入时间。诊断限于请求 ID、模型 ID、状态、耗时与计数，不记录请求体或凭据。

Cloudflare applies its own processing and retention policies. Project non-persistence does not imply that the supplier has no data policy or retention. Cancelling a request cannot guarantee an already-started inference stops.Cloudflare 有自己的处理与保留政策。项目不持久存储，并不意味着供应商没有数据政策或保留行为。取消请求无法保证已经开始的推理停止。
